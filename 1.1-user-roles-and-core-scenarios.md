# Part 1.1: User Roles and Core Scenarios

E-commerce System MVP – Product Manager Assignment

**Approach:** I defined the roles by who acts at each step of the core transaction flow (Browse → Cart/Buy Now → Submit Order → Pay → Ship → Complete). Anything outside that flow is deferred beyond the MVP.

## Role Overview

| Role | Primary Goal | Core Scenarios (MVP) |
|---|---|---|
| **Customer / Buyer** | Find a product quickly and complete the purchase with confidence | • Register / log in (phone or email)<br>• Browse by category and search by keyword<br>• View product details (images, price, specs/SKU, stock status)<br>• Add to cart and edit the cart (quantity, remove)<br>• "Buy Now" for a single item, skipping the cart<br>• Submit an order (confirm items, select or enter shipping address, review total)<br>• Pay through an online payment channel and see the result<br>• Track order status and logistics (Pending Payment, Paid, Shipped, Completed, Cancelled)<br>• Cancel an unpaid order, or request cancellation before shipment<br>• Confirm receipt<br>• View order history and manage the address book |
| **Operations / Merchant Staff** | Keep products accurate and fulfill paid orders on time | • Create and edit products (title, images, description, category, SKU)<br>• Set and change prices<br>• Put products on or off shelf<br>• Manage inventory (view, adjust, low-stock alert)<br>• View and filter the order list (by status, time, order number)<br>• Review paid orders<br>• Arrange shipment: select carrier, enter tracking number, mark as shipped<br>• Handle cancellation requests before shipment |
| **Platform Admin** *(optional, minimal in MVP)* | Keep the platform secure, controlled and observable | • Manage staff accounts and role permissions (RBAC)<br>• View core platform data (orders, GMV, payment success rate, conversion)<br>• Handle abnormal orders (payment succeeded but order not updated, stock conflicts, orders stuck in a status, manual refund or force-close)<br>• Configure basic settings (payment channels, shipping carriers, order auto-cancel timeout) |

## Key Design Decisions

1. **Customer is the priority.** Conversion happens on the customer side, so the browse-to-pay path gets the most polish. Every extra step between "Add to Cart" and "Pay" costs conversions.
2. **Operations is the second priority.** Without products, stock and shipping, no order can be completed. The back office must support the full fulfillment loop, even if the UI is simple.
3. **Admin is kept minimal.** It covers permissions and exception handling, which are the safety net of a transaction system. Dashboards and configuration can be basic or even handled through the database in v1.
4. **Role separation matters for risk control.** Operations staff can change prices and stock but cannot change permissions or force-refund. Those actions stay with the admin, which limits damage from mistakes or misuse.

## Out of Scope for MVP (Planned for Later)

- **Customer:** coupons and promotions, reviews and ratings, wishlist, after-sales and returns, multi-address and invoice management
- **Operations:** bulk import, marketing tools, supplier and purchasing management
- **New roles:** Customer Support (after-sales tickets), Finance (reconciliation and settlement)

**Rationale:** The MVP should validate that the core flow works end to end (a customer can discover, buy and receive a product, and the team can fulfill it) before investing in growth and retention features.
